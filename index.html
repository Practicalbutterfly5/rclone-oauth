rclone

Personal backup client for Google Drive

This page is the application home page for rclone, a personal, single-user Google OAuth client. It exists so that the open-source tool rclone can copy backups from the owner's own servers into the owner's own Google Drive.

What it does

  • Runs a nightly backup of the owner's servers into a folder in the owner's Google Drive.
  • Uploads recorded meeting videos into the owner's Google Drive.
  • Reads those files back when a restore is needed.

Who uses it

Only the owner of this page. The client is not offered to anyone else and has no other users.

Privacy

The application accesses only the owner's own Google Drive and shares nothing with anyone. Details are in the privacy policy.
